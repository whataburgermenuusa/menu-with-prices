What Is on the Whataburger Menu?

The Whataburger menu is broader than most fast food chains at this price point. It runs from classic Whataburgers and Patty Melts to Honey Butter Chicken Biscuits, crispy WhataWings, fresh salads, hearty breakfast options, and hand-spun shakes. Whataburger Sides Menu

Sides at Whataburger are more than an afterthought. Whataburger Drinks and Shakes Menu

Whataburger Customization and Ordering Hacks

Whataburger is built around the idea that you should get what you actually want. Most people don't take full advantage of that. The standard order gets you a good meal. A customized order gets you exactly the meal you were craving. The staff is trained for this, and most modifications cost nothing extra.

Here's what the regulars actually do:

  • Order your burger on Texas Toast instead of the standard bun. It holds better and adds a grilled texture most people prefer.
  • Ask for extra Fancy Ketchup on the side. It's a Whataburger-specific product and genuinely different from standard ketchup.
  • Add a hash brown patty to your burger during breakfast hours. It sounds unusual but adds a satisfying crunch layer.
  • Swap your fries for apple slices in a kids meal or ask at the counter on any order. It's a legitimate option, not a workaround.
  • Use the app before you arrive. You can customize every single modifier before you pull into the parking lot, which saves time and reduces the chance of miscommunication at the window.
  • Ask for green chile on your burger if you're at a location in a state where it's offered. It's a regional add-on that deserves wider availability.
  • Order the Patty Melt with jalapeños added. The combination of the creamy jalapeño notes with Texas Toast is one of the best flavor pairings on the menu.

FAQs - Whataburger Menu

  1. What time does Whataburger serve breakfast?

    Yes. The Kids Whatameal includes a choice of Justaburger, Whatachick'n Bites, Chicken Strips, or Grilled Cheese, with a side and a drink such as milk, chocolate milk, apple juice, or apple slices.

  8. How do Whataburger prices compare to other fast food chains?
    Whataburger sits in the mid-to-upper range of fast food pricing, above McDonald's and Burger King but roughly in line with Shake Shack and Five Guys for comparable items. The portion sizes tend to justify the price difference for most customers.
