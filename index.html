Whataburger Menu — Every Item, Price & Secret You Need to Know

If you've ever pulled into a Whataburger and stared at that menu board longer than you planned, you're not alone. The menu is bigger than most people expect, and there's more going on beneath the surface than a standard burger chain. I've spent years studying fast food menus, and Whataburger consistently stands out for one reason: it gives you genuine options, not just the illusion of them.

This guide covers everything. You'll find every major category, real 2026 price ranges, ordering tips most people miss, and yes — the secret menu items worth actually asking for. Prices vary by location, so treat every figure here as a reliable national average rather than a guarantee.

What Is on the Whataburger Menu?

The Whataburger menu is broader than most fast food chains at this price point. It runs from classic Whataburgers and Patty Melts to Honey Butter Chicken Biscuits, crispy WhataWings, fresh salads, hearty breakfast options, and hand-spun shakes. That range is real, not just marketing. You can eat three different meals here across breakfast, lunch, and dinner and never repeat a category.

What makes the Whataburger menu with prices worth understanding before you visit is the sheer number of customization layers built into every order. You can add jalapeños, grilled onions, or avocado at no extra cost, and every item can be upgraded to a Whatameal — medium fries plus a medium drink — for roughly $3.79 to $4.29 on average in 2026. That's real value if you know to ask.

Whataburger Burger Menu

The burgers are the foundation of this chain, and they've earned that position. The 2026 burger lineup includes the classic Whataburger at $5.59, Double Meat at $6.69, Triple Meat at $8.19, Bacon and Cheese Whataburger at $6.79, Sweet and Spicy Bacon Burger at $7.99, Avocado Bacon Burger at $7.29, and the Patty Melt at $7.89. Each burger comes standard with mustard, lettuce, tomatoes, pickles, and onions on a five-inch bun.

One thing most first-timers miss: ordering the Whataburger Patty Melt on Texas Toast instead of a standard bun changes the entire experience. The bread grills up with a slight crunch that holds the whole thing together better, especially if you're eating in the car. The Jr. versions of most burgers run around $3.69 and are genuinely satisfying if you want something lighter without sacrificing flavor.

Whataburger Chicken Menu

The Whataburger chicken menu gets overlooked by burger-focused visitors, which is a mistake. Options include the Whatachick'n Sandwich at $5.69, Spicy Chicken Sandwich at $5.89, Grilled Chicken Sandwich at $6.39, Whatachick'n Strips at $5.39 for three pieces, and the Honey BBQ Chicken Strip Sandwich at $9.38. The Spicy Chicken Sandwich earns its name without being gimmicky. It has actual heat, not just spice-flavored coating.

A common mistake here is ordering the basic Whatachick'n Sandwich without asking about sauce swaps. The default mayo is fine, but asking for creamy pepper sauce or adding jalapeños changes the profile entirely. Popular chicken options also include Whatachick'n Bites and WhataWings where available, which makes this category more versatile than it first appears on the menu board.

Whataburger Breakfast Menu

Breakfast at Whataburger has a loyal following for good reason, and it runs on its own clock. The Whataburger breakfast menu is available from 11 PM to 11 AM, and standout items include the Honey Butter Chicken Biscuit and Taquito With Cheese. That late-night-to-mid-morning window makes it genuinely useful for shift workers, night owls, and early risers who want something real before 7 AM.

Breakfast items include the Honey Butter Chicken Biscuit at $4.24, Breakfast on a Bun at $3.99, Taquito with Cheese at $3.69, and the Pancake Platter at $6.69. The Honey Butter Chicken Biscuit is consistently the most talked-about breakfast item here. What most people don't realize is that the window closes at 11 AM sharp. If you show up at 11:05, you're getting a burger. Plan accordingly.

Whataburger Sides Menu

Sides at Whataburger are more than an afterthought. The 2026 sides lineup includes French Fries at $1.99 small, $2.19 medium, and $2.39 large, Onion Rings at $3.79, Hash Brown Sticks at $1.99, and Apple Slices at $1.59. That price range makes it easy to add a side without feeling like you're being gouged, which isn't always the case at competing chains.

In my experience, the Whataburger onion rings hold up better than the fries if you're driving or eating on the go. The thick batter keeps them from going soggy as quickly. Fries are excellent fresh, but they have about a five-minute window before they lose that crispness. If you're hitting the drive-through and have any distance to cover, go with onion rings.

Whataburger Drinks and Shakes Menu

The drinks setup here is bigger than you might expect. Small drinks start at 32 oz, and specialty options like Prickly Pear Lemonade run $2.39. The full menu is served 24/7 at most locations, including the complete drinks selection. Hand-spun milkshakes are a genuine highlight — flavors rotate seasonally, and a Salted Caramel Shake runs around $3.69 when available.

One thing worth knowing before you order: adding a shake to your meal adds significant calories that most people don't account for when they're scanning the burger prices. A hand-spun shake can run 700 to 900 calories depending on the size and flavor. That's not a reason to skip it, but it's worth knowing if you're tracking intake. Pair it with a Jr. burger and you'll have a balanced-ish meal rather than an accidental feast.

Whataburger Desserts Menu

Dessert options at Whataburger are simple and honestly executed. The chain doesn't overcomplicate this category, and that's probably the right call. You'll find cookies and pies as the core dessert items, with rotating seasonal additions that show up without much announcement. Availability genuinely varies by location and time of year, so if you're set on a specific dessert, it's worth checking the app before you go.

What I appreciate about the dessert selection here is the lack of pretension. These are straightforward, well-made sweets designed to finish a meal, not upstage it. The cookies in particular are consistently good. If a seasonal shake is available when you visit, that often doubles as the best dessert option on the menu anyway.

Whataburger Kids Menu

The Whataburger kids menu is practical, well-priced, and doesn't talk down to younger diners. Options include the Justaburger Kids Meal at $6.28, Whatachick'n Strips Kids Meal at $6.40, and a Grilled Cheese at $2.99. Parents can swap fries for apple slices or milk for juice. That flexibility matters more than people give it credit for. Not every fast food chain lets you make those swaps without a fight at the counter.

Each Kids Whatameal includes a choice of main item, a side, and a drink such as milk, chocolate milk, apple juice, or apple slices. The Justaburger is a properly sized mini version of the flagship burger, not some diminished version of it. Kids who've eaten here before tend to request it specifically, which says something about the quality consistency across portion sizes.

Whataburger Combo Meals and Prices

Combo meals at Whataburger are called Whatameals, and they're structured simply. You pick your main item, and it comes with medium fries and a medium drink. Upgrading any item to a Whatameal adds roughly $3.79 to $4.29 on average in 2026, depending on the location. One mistake I see regularly: people automatically upgrade to a large combo without checking whether the medium is already enough. The medium fries here are a genuine medium, not a small in disguise.
